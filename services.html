Food Distribution

Regular meal distribution programs ensuring no one in our community goes hungry. We provide nutritious meals to underprivileged families and individuals.

  • Daily meal distribution to needy families
  • Festival special meals & sweet distribution
  • Emergency food supply during natural disasters
  • Nutritional support for malnourished children

Education Support

Scholarships, school supplies, and educational mentoring for underprivileged children. Empowering the next generation through knowledge.

  • Scholarships for deserving students
  • Free school supplies & uniforms
  • After-school tutoring programs
  • Career guidance & mentorship

Health Camps

Free medical check-ups, health awareness programs, and assistance for critical treatments in rural communities.

  • Free general health check-ups
  • Eye & dental screening camps
  • Health awareness workshops
  • Medical assistance for critical patients

Elderly Care

Support and care for senior citizens who lack family support, ensuring they live with dignity and comfort.

  • Regular visits to elderly in need
  • Essential supplies & medicine
  • Companionship & emotional support
  • Assistance with daily necessities

Community Welfare

Clothing drives, emergency relief, festival celebrations, and community development initiatives for a stronger society.

  • Clothing distribution drives
  • Emergency disaster relief
  • Community clean-up events
  • Festival celebrations for underprivileged

Women Empowerment

Skill development, self-help group support, and financial literacy programs for women to achieve independence.

  • Vocational training programs
  • Self-help group formation
  • Financial literacy workshops
  • Micro-enterprise support
